## Choosing a Topic
My decision to choose the topic of ***fishing*** in this project is partially due to a character from my favorite game alongside the fact that I am generally interested in fishing in the future. Although I'm not planning to have fishing as something I do as a possible future job, I figured that it may be fun to do as a hobby or to get my mind off long days while also testing my patience. Although fashion and business were the two runner ups, I find that they aren't as fun as the suspense of catching a fish and finding out what type of speices they are. Some informative websites to help me with my research are ["Fishing Wikipedia"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fishing), ["Fishing Techniques Wikipedia"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fishing_techniques), and ["Global Fishing Watch - Our Technology"](https://globalfishingwatch.org/our-technology/) are the current ones that I have in mind to take a gander at for information. This was pretty intriguing to me as the way to catch fish was advanced over time to make fishing more convenient.


## Engineering Design Process
One of my priorities at the moment is to compare the fishing techniques used from the start and the fishing techniques used today. The major problem is how some people do not understand the impact of overfishing, illegal fishing and habitat destruction caused with certain uses of fishing technology that could be completely avoided.

## Skills
A skill that I learned was how to research and find legitimate sites to aid my research. 

A skill that I am hoping to learn is how I would be able to connect my topic of fishing to major problems in the world that doesn't correspond with fishing while making my presentation positive and intriguing.

## Summary
To conclude, I look forward to creating something that I am genuinely passionate about and willing to do at some point in the future as a side hustle or hobby.
