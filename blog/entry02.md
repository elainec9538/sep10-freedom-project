## Context
The things that I found for fishing were relatively vague and lacking at first, but thanks to switching up the way I searched for information on google, I found various types of hardware and software. Starting off, we looked for the software.
* The first google search I made was **"Fishing Tracker,"** which led me to a website called [*Global Fishing Watch*](https://globalfishingwatch.org/platform/map?latitude=19&longitude=26&zoom=1.49).
  * The site provided data on areas with the most fishing activity with the usage of satellites.
  * It is a tool for spreading awareness on how fishing impacts the ocean and marine life by showing how it impacts the environment, sharing data and analysis all while governing marine life resources.
  * This site is good for providing how people are taking initiative towards the problems of fishing like overfishing.

* The next search was **"Fishing Technology Computer,"** which led me to [*(NOAA Fisheries) National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration Fisheries*](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/national/fisheries-observers/electronic-technologies), a website that explains the usage of electronic tools to collect fishery-dependent data.
  * It discusses things like:
    *  __Electronic Monitoring__ - The use of monitors systems, electronic reports, video cameras, gear sensors, and automated image processing, data collection technologies to improve timeliness, quality and accessibility of data to help fishers obtain more accurate information on fishing.
    *  __Electronic Reporting__ -  More accurate way to collect data from commercial fishing fleets. It used to be jotted down but the creation of tablets and other devices allowed for a more
       * faster,
       * efficient,
       * and collection of accurate data.

##### Reliability and Usefulness (most likely to help you survive if you're stuck on a stranded island)
* Least Reliable to Most Reliable
  * Trapping
    * The usage of traps to catch fish.
    * Takes a lot of patience. 
  * Spearfishing
    * Hunting for fish with spears.
    * Often requires skill and precision.
  * Netting
    * Hauling in a large amount of fish with large nets.
    * Reliability depends on the amount of fish in the area.
* Tied in reliabilty.
  * Hand-gathering, which is gathering sea foods by hand. For example:
    * Picking kelp and shellfish off the beach.
    * Digging for clams or crabs.
    * Reliability varies depending on the type of area you are dealing with. Oftentimes, the sea foods are deeper in the waters. Usually, clams could be found at least five feet in the water with low tides.
  * Angling
    * The usage of a hook attached to a string that often has bait attached to it OR where you use a rod to fish. This is doing by the usage of fishing rods.
* Reliability and Usefulness of Fishing Smart Tech (Least to Most)
  * [Smart Reels](https://i6.govx.net/images/8388185_kastking-ireel-one-amb-smart-fishing-reels_t683.jpg?v=lusx/XyCEJRAImlO5PEa8A==)
    * Even though it lets people see patterns in their fishing skills, it doesn't really do much. Besides, you can use telltale signs of the places that you are fishing at for a while and possibly see the patterns.
  * [Smart Rods](https://i5.walmartimages.com/seo/TACKOBOX-Poletap-Smartrod-Pulse-Spincast-Combo_f6a64eac-85be-492b-99d1-5c8d58da3510.f6fcd4df77988b38e9a835e6e59fe6fc.jpeg?odnHeight=573&odnWidth=573&odnBg=FFFFFF)
    * It tracks the movement of fish and the bites that people may miss. Then again, it depends on how much fish is in the area and if you are using bait to attract the fishes.
  * [Portable Sonars](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/61g8KV0gi+L._AC_SY300_SX300_QL70_FMwebp_.jpg)
    * It does a lot of things like giving us an understanding of the underwater structure, fish activity, depth, and allows you to save locations. It lets you know the places that fishes are more likely to be, which places it at the most useful.

##### Efficiency of the General Fishing Gadgets
* Least to Most Efficient
  * Spearfishing
    * Takes a lot of energy because you are throwing it into the water to hit the fish. Not the most efficient now, but before? Sure.
  * Trapping
    * Takes patience and time for something to get trapped.
  * Angling
    * One fish at a time.
  * Netting
    * Good for catching a large amount of fish.
  * Hand-gathering
    * Guarantees that you have sea food at the end of the day.
   With fish attractors, the position of angling would be swapped with hand-gathering.
