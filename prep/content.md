This search led me to [_NOAA Fisheries_](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/national/fisheries-observers/electronic-technologies), a website that explains the usage of electronic tools to collect fishery-dependent data.
  * __Electronic monitoring__, basically to help fishers catch fish with more accurate information at the right times with the usage of:
    * Monitors systems, electronic reports, video cameras, gear sensors, and automated image processing, data collection technologies to improve timeliness, quality and accessibility of data.
  * __Electronic Reporting__, a more accurate way to collect data from commercial fishing fleets and send it to NOAA Fisheries and state marine resource agencies.
    * Fishery-dependent data used to be recorded using paper and logbooks until today, where it is being transitioned to tablets and other devices for a much more
      * Faster
      * Efficient
      * Collection of accurate data
  * This allows for people in the fishing industry to get more information before they fish.

* Least Reliable to Most Reliable
  * Spearfishing
    * Hunting for fish with spears.
    * Often requires skill and precision.
  * Netting
    * Hauling in a large amount of fish with large nets.
    * Reliability depends on the amount of fish in the area.
* Tied in reliabilty.
  * Hand-gathering, which is gathering sea foods by hand. For example:
    * Picking kelp and shellfish off the beach.
    * Digging for clams or crabs.
    * Reliability varies depending on the type of area you are dealing with. Oftentimes, the sea foods are deeper in the waters. Usually, clams could be found at least five feet in the water with low tides.
  * Angling
    * The usage of a hook attached to a string that often has bait attached to it OR where you use a rod to fish. This is doing by the usage of fishing rods.
